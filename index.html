Earmark
GitHub ↗

What if your Spotify listening history could tell you what to read next?

A Spotify-integrated book recommendation engine that analyzes your listening history across three time ranges — building a weighted taste profile from your top artists — and uses two distinct recommendation engines: a rule-based genre mapper and a Claude LLM engine that reasons about mood, themes, and cultural associations in natural language. Users can rate recommendations to improve future suggestions.
